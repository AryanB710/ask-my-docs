# Service Level Agreement

This SLA describes LumenQueue's monthly uptime commitment and the service credits you receive if we miss it.

## Uptime commitment

The Standard tier has a monthly uptime commitment of 99.9%. The Enterprise tier has a monthly uptime commitment of 99.99%. The Free tier has no SLA.

Uptime is measured per region as the percentage of one-minute intervals in which the API returned successful responses to at least 95% of valid requests.

## Service credits

If monthly uptime falls below the commitment, you are eligible for a service credit:

- Uptime below the commitment but at or above 99.0%: credit of 10% of the monthly bill.
- Uptime below 99.0% but at or above 95.0%: credit of 25% of the monthly bill.
- Uptime below 95.0%: credit of 50% of the monthly bill.

Service credits are applied to future invoices and are never paid out as cash.

## How to claim

Credit claims must be submitted through a support ticket within 30 days of the end of the month in which the incident occurred. Include the affected region and the times of the failed requests.

## Exclusions

The SLA does not apply to downtime caused by scheduled maintenance announced at least 72 hours in advance, by your own exceeding of rate limits, or by projects suspended for non-payment.
